# Trailway Systems product hub

A static, mobile-first catalog linking visitors to the available format and store for each record-keeping product. No frameworks, scripts, font requests, analytics or email capture.

## Files
- `index.html` — public single-page catalog, CSS is local in `assets/style.css`.
- `assets/` — optimized, locally hosted owner-approved gravel-road brand art.
- `catalog.json` — internal link/source manifest; not required to render the page.
- `VERIFICATION.md` and `reports/` — link checks, source limitations and local QA notes.

## Publishing
Hosted free with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch of the user-site repository. No paid services or custom domain. Serve `index.html` and `assets/`; no build step is needed.

## Product records
Only documented product destinations are used. Unknown Amazon ASINs are not guessed. Coin paperback, Dog hardcover and Household paperback are marked coming soon, with no purchase link. Business Mileage and Rental Property are named only as forthcoming print editions, not offered as products. RV remains lower on the page, with its existing offers, and no claim of new releases.

Payhip exact product permalinks were not available in local records or in the publicly readable storefront, so nine buttons explicitly open the live storefront and tell the visitor to pick the named title. Each has a distinct product-level UTM content value. Firearm is not linked to Payhip.

Art credit: optimized local copy of `/workspace/products/x-profile/pfp-1-gravel.jpg`, owner-selected gravel-road brand asset. No external requests for imagery or fonts.
