# Alternatives to the Trailway Systems GitHub Pages hub

Recommendation: **use the GitHub Pages hub as the one public bio link.** Keep the existing Gumroad and Payhip stores as sales channels, not as substitutes for the overall catalog. All options below assume no new subscription or custom domain.

| Option | Multi-store coverage | Branding and layout | Speed | Tracking | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GitHub Pages (recommended)** | Excellent: the same product card can point to Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip and direct Amazon paperback/hardcover editions. Bundles and unavailable formats can be labeled accurately. | Strongest here: fully owned warm sunset/gravel-road styling, arena order, copy and local art. No required platform badge. | Lightweight static HTML/CSS, no runtime scripts, local images, system fonts. | Product/store UTM tags only; no added analytics scripts, email capture or trackers. Click reporting depends on each destination’s UTM support. | Free hosting on the existing public account. No marketplace fee added by this hub. Public source, repository administration needed. |
| **Free Linktree** | Good for an outbound list, but grouping every format and every store produces many repeated links rather than a clear product-format catalog. | Fast to operate, but free-plan theme/layout and platform branding are limited compared with a purpose-built page. | Simple visitor experience, though still a third-party link-page shell. | Destination UTMs can be copied into URLs manually. Linktree has visitor analytics; automatic UTM controls are restricted to paid tiers. | Free plan is $0; advanced design, automatic UTM controls and deeper analytics are upsells. |
| **Payhip storefront** | Not sufficient: it is a Payhip sales catalog, not a hub for Etsy or Amazon formats. Firearm is deliberately excluded; RV is not fully represented as individual listings here. | Storefront layout, content and assets can be customized, but cards and grouping are controlled by Payhip. Its placeholder About text indicates brand presentation still needs work. | One storefront step, but account-hosted dynamic pages, less lean and less controllable than static HTML. | Built-in daily views/sales and product sales breakdown. Optional Google Analytics integration is available and should stay off under the no-tracker rule. Check destination UTM attribution before relying on it. | Free Forever plan $0/month; 5% fee per sale on this plan, plus payment-processor economics. |
| **Gumroad profile** | Strongest of the three for the existing digital range and all three Gumroad bundles, but not for Etsy-only RV products or Amazon’s bound editions. | Creator profile rather than a universal arena-based catalog; no direct per-product alternative-store matrix. | Straightforward product browsing with hosted dynamic pages and platform assets. | Store-native reporting and any optional platform tracking/integrations; UTM behavior should be verified for per-product source reporting. | No monthly fee; official pricing is 10% + $0.50 per profile/direct sale (30% Discover). No new hosting cost. |

**Bottom line:** Linktree is adequate if the sole goal is a quick list of links; Payhip and Gumroad are adequate if the goal is only sales within those individual stores. None replaces one lightweight, branded page that can accurately present all formats and all external stores. GitHub Pages best matches that goal at $0, with control over disclaimers, availability and missing-ASIN handling.

Sources: official [Linktree paid-features comparison](https://help.linktr.ee/en/articles/5434140-an-overview-of-paid-features-available-on-linktree), [Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing), [Payhip sales and views](https://help.payhip.com/article/109-sales-and-views), [Payhip Google Analytics](https://help.payhip.com/article/93-google-analytics), and existing Payhip-plan notes in `/workspace/products/bundles/README.md`.
